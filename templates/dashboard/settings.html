New order emails

Every time an order is paid, an email with what was bought and where to deliver it goes to these addresses.

Service charge

The service charge is added at checkout on the price of the goods (after any promo code), not on delivery. Set 0 to turn it off.

Delivery rates

Customers choose their state at checkout. A state with no line of its own below uses "Bus delivery outside Ondo"; "I will pick it up" uses Pickup. Tick "Customer pays the driver" and checkout charges nothing for delivery on that rate.

Delivery for each state

Every state is listed. With "Pay driver" ticked, the customer pays the bus driver on arrival and you charge nothing at checkout. Untick it and type a fee to charge that state yourself, as Ondo does. Press Save state fees once when you are done. Switch a state off to stop sending there.
